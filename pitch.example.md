# Business and Funding Pitch

Replace this outline with your own free-form description before running.

Describe your business, the problem it solves, and whom it serves. Explain your
product, business model, and why your team can deliver it.

Include verified traction, market information, and financial figures if useful.
State how much funding you seek, how you plan to use it, and the kind of investor
you hope to work with. Include your name and a clear request for a conversation.

Only provide facts you want used in your outreach; omit anything confidential that
you do not want sent to the configured LLM service.
